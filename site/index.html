How to use DubTool in 4 simple steps

No programming or extra installs. Set up once in about two minutes.

  1. 01Unzip and open DubTool

    Extract the Windows zip and double-click DubTool.exe. Everything is built in.

  2. 02Paste a free translation key

    Open Settings → API Keys, paste a Google or Groq key, and click Test all.

  3. 03Drop your video and pick a voice

    Choose an output folder, leave Auto-detect on, and preview any local voice.

  4. 04Click Generate for video & Word script

    Your original stays untouched; you get a new Vietnamese MP4 and Word table.

Already in Vietnamese? DubTool detects it automatically, stops before using any key, and offers clean subtitles instead.

Built to be simple and reliable. Your video never leaves your computer.

Common questions about DubTool

Plain answers about cost, PC requirements, privacy, and current limits before you download.

Ask a question on GitHub

Is DubTool completely free and without watermarks?

Yes. DubTool is free, open-source software (MIT license) with no video length limit and no logos stamped on your exported video.

Does DubTool need a gaming PC or graphics card?

No. DubTool runs directly on a normal Windows CPU. If your PC has less memory, switch to the lightweight Nano voice mode in Settings.

Is it hard to get a free Google or Groq translation key?

No. Sign in to Google AI Studio or Groq Console, click Create API Key, copy the text, and paste it into DubTool Settings once. It takes about two minutes.

Will my original video be uploaded to the internet?

Never. Your video and audio stay on your computer. Only the short transcribed text lines are sent for translation, or nothing at all if you choose Ollama.

Does DubTool work on Mac or require installing Python?

Right now DubTool is for Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit) only, and you do not need to install Python or FFmpeg. Just unzip and run DubTool.exe.
